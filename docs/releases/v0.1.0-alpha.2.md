# Orbit Voice 0.1.0-alpha.2

Second alpha. Speech on the watch is now recognized offline on the paired phone with
a bundled multilingual Whisper model instead of the Android speech service, so
Russian speech stays Russian and no recording leaves the phone.

## Included

- Offline multilingual speech recognition on the phone: whisper.cpp with the
  multilingual `base` model and Silero voice-activity detection. Model weights are
  fetched at build time from pinned revisions and verified by SHA-256 before they are
  packaged; licenses ship inside the phone APK.
- Watch microphone audio streamed to the phone over an ephemeral Wear OS
  ChannelClient connection with a versioned header. Recordings stay in memory and are
  discarded after the turn; only recognized text becomes part of the conversation.
- A watch speech endpoint measured against the device's own microphone floor: it
  starts an utterance after roughly 50 ms of speech, ends it after about 0.8 s of
  silence, and stops waiting after 30 s of silence instead of listening forever.
- Speech language options: **Automatic** (offline detection), the **system speech
  service**, or one fixed language for the installed recognizer. Use Automatic for
  Russian, or pick the matching system language.
- A tag-triggered release workflow that requires this file, validates the tag against
  `versionName`, verifies the signing certificate and publishes the APKs.

## Install

Install the **phone APK on an Android 11+ phone** and the **wear APK on a Wear OS
watch running Android API 30+**, then pair them through the normal manufacturer/Wear
OS companion. Both APKs must come from this release; they share the application ID
for Data Layer communication and must not be installed together on one device.

The phone APK is larger than the watch APK because it carries the speech model.

On the phone, choose **Continue with ChatGPT**, complete the eligible account/plan
consent flow and select a model, then leave **Speech language** on **Automatic** for
offline multilingual recognition. On the watch, use **Options → Refresh connection**,
then **Start talking**.

## Verification and alpha limits

- 38 JVM tests passed (`:core:test`).
- 26 instrumented tests passed on a physical Pixel 9 Pro XL (Android 17), covering
  the offline audio pipeline, Russian → English → Russian switching on recorded
  samples, silence handling, cancellation, encrypted storage and phone UI.
- The speech endpoint shipped here is covered by regression tests that fail against
  the 0.1.0-alpha.1 threshold logic.
- Both lint tasks and signed release builds passed. Both APKs carry the same signing
  certificate as 0.1.0-alpha.1.
- On physical devices, Russian speech recorded on a Pixel Watch 3 was transcribed to
  Cyrillic on the phone and displayed on the watch, and the same phrase spoken into
  the phone was transcribed correctly with the speech language set to Automatic.
- Measured on a Pixel 9 Pro XL: 1.92 s of audio decoded in 2.30 s (base model,
  4 threads); watch to phone audio transfer was about 1.2 s; the speech model loads
  from disk in roughly 0.3 s.

**Still unverified:** a complete spoken dialogue including the assistant's spoken
answer, mid-conversation Russian ↔ English switching on the watch, screen-off and
lock behaviour, Tile host rendering, and latency or battery measurements across a
longer conversation. The account's plan limit was reached during the physical-device
check, so the answer half of the loop is untested in this build. Account
eligibility, plan limits and device background policy still apply.

History stays on the phone and does not appear in ChatGPT. This release does not
implement native ChatGPT Voice or Realtime Audio through the account grant.
